Built for the moment patients forget

Discharge instructions are often misunderstood right when they matter most. Care Companion translates clinical language into something a patient can actually act on.

Escalation stays rule-based

Symptom severity is triaged by a deterministic rule engine, not left to a model — generative AI handles the wording, not the safety-critical decision.

A dashboard that stays current

Every check-in updates the care team's view automatically, so follow-up calls happen where they're actually needed, not everywhere at once.
